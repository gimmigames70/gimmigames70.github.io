Orbit Hop privacy policy

Gimme Games · Effective 1 November 2026

This policy explains what information is collected when you play Orbit Hop, a mobile game made by Gimme Games (“we”, “us”), and how it is used. If you have any questions, email gimmigames70@gmail.com.

What we collect ourselves

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address or other personal details through the game. Your best score and the comets you have unlocked are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us, and they are deleted if you uninstall the game or clear its data.

Advertising

Orbit Hop is free to play and shows ads through Google AdMob, a service provided by Google. To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect and process information from your device, including:

  • your device’s advertising ID and other device identifiers;
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • information about your device, such as its model, operating system and language;
  • how you interact with ads, such as whether an ad was viewed or tapped.

Google uses this information under its own privacy policy. You can read how Google uses information from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your choices about ads

If you are in the UK, the European Economic Area or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. If you decline, you may still see ads, but they will not be based on your interests. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android device’s settings under Google › Ads or Privacy › Ads.

Legal basis

Where UK or EU data protection law applies, personalised advertising is based on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time through your device settings. Non-personalised advertising and basic ad measurement are based on our legitimate interest in keeping the game free to play.

Children

Orbit Hop is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data held about you, or to object to how it is used. Because we do not store personal data ourselves, most requests are best directed to Google, but we are happy to help. If you are in the UK, you can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the game handles information, we will update this page and the date at the top.
